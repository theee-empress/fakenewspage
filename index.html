SILICON VALLEY, CA — For decades, couples, roommates, and coworkers have relied on outdated conflict-resolution methods like "active listening," "compromise," and "evidence." Today, a new Silicon Valley startup is disrupting the interpersonal communication space by asking a bold question: What if you’re just right because you’re louder? Enter EchoRage, the world’s first AI-powered argument settler designed to declare the angriest person in the room the undisputed winner of any debate. "People are exhausted by losing arguments just because the other person happens to have 'facts' or 'basic logic' on their side," said EchoRage founder and CEO Brad Sterling during a tense, red-faced press conference on Tuesday. "We realized that what consumers actually want isn't resolution. It's pure, unadulterated victory."
