New Smartphone App Solves Arguments by Agreeing With Whoever Is Angrier

Meet EchoRage, the revolutionary conflict-resolution platform that replaces "facts" and "logic" with pure, unadulterated decibel validation.

By Tyler Vance, Tech Correspondent | November 12, 2026

[EchoRage v1.0 User Interface Preview]

EchoRage’s flagship interface ignores logical fallacies and listens strictly for peak vocal cord strain. (Photo: EchoRage Press Kit)

SILICON VALLEY, CA — For decades, couples, roommates, and coworkers have relied on outdated conflict-resolution methods like "active listening," "compromise," and "evidence." Today, a new Silicon Valley startup is disrupting the interpersonal communication space by asking a bold question: What if you’re just right because you’re louder?

Enter EchoRage, the world’s first AI-powered argument settler designed to declare the angriest person in the room the undisputed winner of any debate.

"People are exhausted by losing arguments just because the other person happens to have 'facts' or 'basic logic' on their side," said EchoRage founder and CEO Brad Sterling during a tense, red-faced press conference on Tuesday. "We realized that what consumers actually want isn't resolution. It's pure, unadulterated victory."

How It Works: Decibel-Based Truth™

Unlike traditional mediation tools that attempt to find middle ground, EchoRage utilizes proprietary Decibel-Based Validation technology. When a disagreement breaks out, users simply place their phone on the table and tap the big red "I'M NOT EVEN MAD, YOU'RE MAD" button.

From there, the app’s algorithm ignores all context, historical accuracy, and common sense, focusing entirely on three key metrics:

  • Peak Volume: Measured in decibels. Anything over 95 dB automatically triggers a "Fact-Check Override."
  • Vocal Strain: Advanced sensors detect neck-vein bulging and vocal cord fraying to calculate emotional commitment.
  • Interruptions Per Minute (IPM): The app rewards users who successfully talk over their opponent without taking a breath.

Once a threshold of fury is reached, EchoRage sounds an airhorn, flashes strobe lights, and announces the winner in a booming voice: "CONGRATULATIONS! YOU ARE COMPLETELY RIGHT AND THEY ARE BEING HYSTERICAL!"

Ending Compromise Forever

Early beta testers report a 100% increase in personal vindication, alongside a 400% spike in slammed doors and silent car rides.

"My husband tried to show me a bank statement to prove I spent too much on throw pillows," said beta tester Brenda Miller, 34. "Normally, that would be the end of it. Instead, I opened EchoRage, screamed 'YOU NEVER SUPPORT MY DREAMS' at 104 decibels, and the app immediately generated a legally non-binding certificate proving he owed me an apology. It’s a marriage saver."

Pricing and Availability

EchoRage is available now for iOS and Android. The free tier allows users to win up to three minor petty squabbles per month. For $19.99/month, users can upgrade to EchoRage Platinum, which unlocks unlimited screaming matches, automatic screenshot receipts of your opponent's "calm" demeanor to use against them later, and a "Bring Up Something From Three Years Ago" emergency boost button.
